# The Quacks of Quedlinburg — Base Game Rulebook

> **Transcription source**: Compiled from the official rulebook PDF (Wolfgang Warsch / Schmidt Spiele 2018), UltraBoardGames.com, BoardGameGeek, multiple rules-reference sites, and training knowledge. Values marked `[VERIFY]` should be cross-checked against the physical rulebook.

---

## 1. Overview

**Players**: 2–4 (5 with Herb Witches expansion)  
**Rounds**: 9  
**Win condition**: Most Victory Points (VP) after 9 rounds  
**Core mechanics**: Bag-building + push-your-luck

Players are quack doctors brewing miracle potions. Each round you draw ingredient chips from your bag and place them in your cauldron, hoping to advance as far as possible without your potion **exploding**. After each round you spend coins to buy better ingredients, expanding and improving your bag.

## 4. Round Structure

Each of the **9 rounds** follows this sequence:

```
[Fortune Teller] → [Pulling Phase] → [Evaluation A–E]
```

### 4.1 Fortune Teller Phase (Start of Round)
Reveal the top Fortune Teller card. Read it aloud. Apply its effect (see Section 8).

- **Purple cards**: Immediate one-time effect
- **Blue/light cards**: Effect lasts the entire round

### 4.2 Pulling Phase (Simultaneous)
All players draw chips from their bags **simultaneously and independently**.

**On your turn (repeatable):**
1. Reach into your bag without looking, draw **one chip**
2. Place it in the cauldron spiral starting from after the droplet position (or after the rat stone position if rat stones are ahead of droplet)
3. Each chip's **value** determines how many spaces it advances from the previous chip
4. Decide: **continue drawing** or **stop voluntarily**

**Explosion rule:** If the **sum of all white chip values** in your cauldron **exceeds 7**, your pot **explodes**. You immediately stop drawing.

> Example: White 3 + White 2 + White 2 = 7. Still safe. Drawing another White 1 = 8. EXPLOSION.

**Flask rule:** Once per round, after drawing any chip, you may use your flask to immediately **return that chip to your bag**. The chip was never placed. The flask is now empty for the rest of this round.

**Yellow chip rule:** If the chip drawn is a Yellow chip, AND the **immediately preceding drawn chip** was White, you may put that White chip back in the bag (without spending the flask).

**Blue chip rule** [Book variant]: When a Blue chip is drawn, look at (chip value) chips from bag; place one of them OR return all.

### 4.3 Cauldron Position
Your **cauldron position** = the space in the spiral where your last-placed chip ended up.

- Minimum starting position: your **droplet position** (advances over game)
- Maximum position: **space 33** (chips that would advance beyond 33 are capped at 33)
- Spaces with ruby symbols: collect 1 ruby immediately when you land on one

## 5. Evaluation Phases

### Phase A: Scoring (VP)
All players move their **scoring marker** forward by the VP value corresponding to their cauldron position (see scoring table in Section 9).

**Explosion penalty:** If you exploded, you must choose **either** VP advancement **or** coin income (not both).

When your scoring marker passes over or lands on **ruby symbols on the scoring track**, you collect those rubies.

### Phase B: Special Ingredient Effects
Resolve ingredient effects that trigger during evaluation:

- **Green chips** (Book 1): If sum of white chips in pot = **exactly 7**, advance your last chip forward by the total value of all green chips in the pot
- **Black chips**: Count black chips in pot for special effect (see Section 7)
- **Purple chips**: Determine upgrade tier for buying phase (see Section 7)

### Phase C: Rat Stones (Catch-Up Mechanic)
1. Identify the player with the **highest scoring marker** position (the leader)
2. Every **other** player counts how many spaces their marker is **behind** the leader
3. Each trailing player places their rat stone marker that many spaces **ahead of their droplet** in the cauldron
4. These rat stones serve as the starting advance in next round's pulling phase

> Example: Leader is at space 14. You are at space 10. Your rat stone goes 4 spaces past your droplet.

### Phase D: Buying (The Market)
Each player spends coins to purchase new ingredient chips. Coins earned = your cauldron position number (except position 33 = 35 coins [VERIFY]).

**If you exploded**: If you chose coins over VP, use those coins now.

**Buying rules:**
- You may buy **multiple chips** per round (pay cost, take chip, put in bag immediately)
- Each chip has a fixed cost (see Section 7 for ingredient costs)
- Stock is limited; once a chip type/value sells out it's gone [VERIFY stock quantities]
- **Purple chip upgrade** (if purple chips in pot): may trade chips from bag (see Purple ability, Section 7)

### Phase E: End of Round Preparation
1. **Ruby spending** (optional, any number of times):
   - Spend **2 rubies** → advance droplet 1 space forward in cauldron
   - Spend **2 rubies** → refill flask (flask becomes available for next round)
2. **White chip addition** (Round 6 only): each player adds 1 White 1-chip to their bag
3. **Next Fortune Teller card**: reveal for next round
4. All players **return all chips from cauldron to bag** for next round

## 7. Ingredients

### 7.1 White (Cherry Bombs)
- **Role**: Explosion trigger
- **Chips available**: 1, 2, 3, 4
- **Effect**: Each white chip advances the cauldron position by its value. If total white chip values in pot > 7 → **explosion**
- **Not purchasable** (you start with them; 1 White 1-chip is added to each bag in Round 6)
- **Costs**: N/A

### 7.2 Orange (Pumpkin / Nightshade)
- **Role**: Filler chip; enables Red chip bonus
- **Chips available**: 1 (value = 1)

## 10. End of Game

After Round 9, evaluate:
1. Each player converts remaining **rubies** → VP: every 2 rubies = 1 VP [VERIFY]
2. Remaining **coins** do NOT count toward VP
3. Highest total VP wins
4. Tiebreaker: most coins remaining [VERIFY]

## 11. Key Rules Summary

| Situation | Rule |
|---|---|
| White sum = 7 | Safe — keep drawing or stop |
| White sum > 7 | Explosion — stop immediately, choose VP or coins |
| Flask used | Flask now empty; costs 2 rubies to refill |
| Ruby space hit | Collect ruby immediately |
| Reached space 33 | Cap there; earn 15 VP and 35 coins |
| Rat stones | Non-leaders get stones = gaps between their score and leader's score |
| Droplet advance | Spend 2 rubies → move droplet 1 space in spiral (persists every round) |
